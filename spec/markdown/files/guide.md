# A short guide to sourdough

Sourdough is bread raised by wild yeast and bacteria instead of baker's yeast. It takes longer, but it keeps better and tastes of something.

![A loaf, cut open](images/loaf.jpg "The crumb of a good loaf")

## The starter

A starter is flour and water that's been left to ferment. To make one:

3. Mix 50 g of wholemeal flour with 50 g of water in a jar.
4. Leave it somewhere warm, loosely covered, for a day.
5. Throw half away and feed it again with the same amounts.

Keep going for a week. It's ready when it doubles within six hours of a feed and smells sour rather than ~~cheesy~~ off.

> **Tip:** rye flour starts faster than wheat.
>
> > Some bakers swear by pineapple juice for the first feed. It works, but water does too.

## The bake

| Step | Time | Temperature |
| :--- | ---: | :---: |
| Mix and rest | 1 h | room |
| Bulk ferment | 4 to 6 h | 24 °C |
| Shape and prove | overnight | fridge |
| Bake | 45 min | 250 °C |

Bake in a covered pot for the first 20 minutes, then take the lid off. The loaf is done when it sounds hollow underneath.
